# 4 Look at Tolaria

The same files, a tool built around them. Structure, views, an interface that helps.

Goal: you know whether you want more than Obsidian.

## Steps

- [ ] Open tolaria.md and download Tolaria.
- [ ] Open a copy of this folder in it. The files stay plain Markdown, nothing to migrate.
- [ ] Look at three things: types (what kind of note is this), relationships (which note belongs to which), views (saved lists, such as all open projects).
- [ ] Ask yourself: would I find things faster here, or is Obsidian enough?

## Done when

- [ ] You decided: Obsidian, Tolaria, or both on the same folder.

Better tools will keep coming. The files are yours, so you can switch at any time. Feeding is the next job, whatever the tool.
