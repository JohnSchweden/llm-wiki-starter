# 1 Feed it

Clip, dictate, paste. Digest every evening. A wiki grows by eating.

Goal: after one week, `wiki/log.md` shows seven ingests.

## Every day

- [ ] Put one thing into `raw/`. Pick one:
  - Clip a web page with the Obsidian Web Clipper. Its folder is `raw/`.
  - Dictate a thought with Wispr Flow into a new note in `raw/`.
  - Paste text you wrote anyway: meeting notes, a mail, a memo. Leave out what may not leave the company.
- [ ] In the evening, type: "Digest everything new in raw/ into the wiki."
- [ ] Read the summary the agent gives you. Fix what it got wrong, in your own words, as a new note in `raw/`.

## Once this week

- [ ] Ask the wiki a question you would otherwise ask a colleague: "What do I know about …?" Open the article it cites and check the answer against it.
- [ ] Ask one it cannot know. It should say so.
- [ ] Write one piece from the wiki: "Write a LinkedIn post about … from the wiki." The draft lands in `outputs/`.
- [ ] Feed it back: "Ingest that output into the wiki."

## Done when

- [ ] `wiki/log.md` lists seven ingests from this week.
